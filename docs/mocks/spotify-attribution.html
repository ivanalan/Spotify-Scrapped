Read of the board

  • Shipped: A (cover → Spotify, no chip/chrome credit) + B (row links + list caption) + C (stage credit outside capture).
  • B: row link-out is enough for lists; “Open on Spotify” as row subline may be too loud. Try artist name as subline and keep the row as the link.

  • Avoid Spotify green fills and logo-as-Scrapped-chrome. Credit is content provenance, not co-branding.
