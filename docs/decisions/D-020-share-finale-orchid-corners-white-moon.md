# D-020: Share finale: orchid corners + white moon (spine)

**Status:** Locked (spine): production promote [D-021](D-021-share-finale-teal-orchid-lilac-moon.md)  
**Date:** 2026-10-20  
**Surfaces:** Share-finale visual art. IA + density stay [D-012](D-012-share-finale-inset.md). Composition rules stay [D-013](D-013-share-finale-deck-rhyme-explore.md).

## Decision

Lock the next share-card art direction to this spine and discard competing card-field kits:

1. **Card field**: ink nest (`#1C1828` → `#12101A`) + **B1 orchid corners** wash (orchid-deep TL · orchid-ink BR): same recipe as left-on-read [D-019](D-019-left-on-read-orchid-corners.md), on the share island (not the stage).
2. **Moon**: BL corner sphere (center on card corner, sphere only) but **whiter** than D-014’s gold→mango→rose: paper / pearl / cool-white family.
3. **Ghosts**: keep framing blobs (TL + BR of hero); eyes tuned for ink card.
4. **Stage**: unchanged bookend: ink + intro disks/star (D-014 stage).

Further work only **tunes** this spine: **card base gradient** (nest under the wash), moon stops, wash strength/spread, moon size, ghost opacity. Do not reopen four-corner bloom, disk frags, or competing compositions that drop orchid corners or the white moon.

## Why

User chose orchid corners **with** a moon in one composition; not corners alone, not teal+wash hybrids. A warm mango/rose moon fights the orchid wash; a whiter moon reads as a second quiet light against purple corners without re-heating the card into volume-ember territory.
